# Limitations

## Synthetic Data Does Not Establish Investment Validity

Every holding, asset, price, return, sector, region, currency, and shock is generated for demonstration. The observed relationships are designed to exercise the application; they are not evidence of real portfolio performance, market behavior, valuation accuracy, or investment suitability.

## No Investment Advice or Portfolio Action

The application must not be used to select, buy, sell, hedge, price, value, allocate, approve, or monitor a real investment portfolio. It does not constitute investment advice, a risk limit, a valuation, a suitability assessment, a capital calculation, or a substitute for professional judgment.

## Simplified Holdings and Valuation

The holdings model uses generic fields and compatible synthetic values. It does not incorporate instrument terms, issuer hierarchies, derivatives, funds-through-funds, accrued interest, dividends, coupons, corporate actions, tax effects, collateral, leverage, financing, liquidity, or controlled FX conversion. Real portfolios require authoritative position, pricing, and reference-data controls.

## Historical Risk Metrics Are Descriptive

Volatility, drawdown, VaR, CVaR, Sharpe, and beta-like statistics are calculated from a limited synthetic history. They depend on return sampling, confidence level, horizon, weights, and assumptions. They cannot predict future losses or cover all market, liquidity, credit, model, operational, legal, or concentration risks.

## Scenario Simplification

Scenarios use deterministic static shocks. They do not capture correlations, nonlinear payoffs, convexity, volatility-surface changes, liquidity haircuts, bid/ask spreads, market impact, counterparty behavior, dynamic rebalancing, hedging effectiveness, or second-order effects. A positive or negative scenario result therefore cannot be treated as a forecast or capital estimate.

## Input Checks Are Not Full Data Governance

Required-field, weight, duplicate, price, and market-value checks are useful guardrails but do not prove source accuracy, completeness, entitlement, licensing, lineage, point-in-time consistency, or reconciliation to books and records. Local uploads are demonstration inputs only.

## Local Application Constraints

The project is a single-user local app. It does not provide authentication, authorization, encrypted storage, audit-grade logging, approvals, model registry, market-data licensing controls, scheduled reports, limit monitoring, or operational resilience. A real implementation would need a substantially broader control environment.

## Human and Organizational Accountability

Any production implementation would require accountable investment, risk, operations, data, legal, compliance, security, and model-governance stakeholders. Documentation and visualizations help make assumptions visible, but they do not replace approved methodology, independent review, or formal risk governance.
